UN1CA 3.2.0 for Galaxy M51

What's new
- Added built-in OTA update support for the Galaxy M51.
- Future ROM updates can now be checked through UN1CA Updates.
- Added M51 compatibility adjustments for Google voice and Hotword components.
- Updated SoundBooster and SoundAlive compatibility.
- Added edge-lighting model compatibility.
- Verified dirty-flash upgrade from UN1CA 3.1.0.
- Improved overall M51 integration and stability.
